# Light Wave: portfolio review and editorial rationale

The revised page is a retrospective account of the project, using the original
description, exhibition photographs, and your account of the fabrication and
audience encounter. It is saved locally for review.

## The strongest narrative

The project expresses your perception of the artificial and the organic through
programming and artistic judgment. You identified existentialism as its
philosophical lens. The floating-screen intention gives that inquiry a spatial
form, informed by observation in NYC parks.
Its material development gives that connection substance: translucent fabric
thread had to function visually and remain securely stretched within a custom
metal frame. Visitors then encountered the changing light and sound, asked
about the medium, and wanted to understand the project’s meaning.

The narrative therefore moves through:

1. **Artistic inquiry:** a floating screen that adds a sensory layer to an
   existing space, expressing a personal inquiry into perception and meaning.
   Colors sampled in NYC parks and intentionally edited demo audio contribute
   sensory material.
2. **Material investigation:** experiments with thread sizes and materials.
3. **Frame decision and fabrication challenge:** choosing lightweight metal
   under time and weight constraints, connecting the thread to LED strips, and maintaining
   stable tension between the top and bottom of the frame.
4. **Exhibition observations:** curiosity about the changing light, sound,
   material, patterns, and underlying idea.
5. **Critical reflection:** how to communicate the artistic inquiry and
   document material choices more clearly in another presentation.

This explains your decisions without padding the project with an invented
development history.

## Evidence and interpretation

**Documented:** colors sampled in NYC parks; exhibition at the 2022 ITP Spring
Show; photographs and a demonstration video. Your correction establishes that
the sound came from demo audio in free sound resources, which you intentionally
edited, rather than from your own field recordings.

**Confirmed by you:** Arduino, LED strips, translucent fabric thread, a custom
metal frame, thread size/material experiments, connection and tension challenges,
the audience observations described above, the floating-screen intention,
the choice of lightweight metal under time and weight constraints, and your
existential lens and emphasis on programming as part of artistic practice.

**Editorial interpretation:** framing the piece through the relationship between
organic expression and engineered structure, and reading visitors’ questions as
an opportunity to communicate the concept more clearly. These appear as
retrospective analysis rather than measured findings.

**Proposed future work:** systematic thread comparisons and investigation of
ambient light, sound balance, and viewing distance. The page identifies these as
next steps, not experiments already completed.

The revision makes no claims about a particular Arduino model, thread diameter,
attachment method, software, light-to-sound mapping, visitor counts, or measured
emotional outcomes.

## Philosophical framing

The existential language is a personal artistic lens, confirmed by you. The
page discusses making, encountering, and interpreting a work; it does not claim
that natural-looking light establishes a philosophical theory or that an
object has the same kind of existence as a person. For editorial background,
see the [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy’s account of existentialism](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/fall2025/entries/existentialism/).

## What would strengthen the documentation next

Your initial sketches would establish the floating-screen intention visually.
Pairing one with a photo of the final installation would connect the spatial
idea to the material and frame decisions.

Another valuable addition would be one comparison photograph of the threads
you tested, accompanied by a short explanation of what changed and why you
selected the final material. A close-up or sketch of the LED/thread connection
would help a reviewer understand the central fabrication challenge.

If you retain a visitor’s actual words, a short attributed observation would
support the exhibition reflection. The current writing uses your recollection
and presents it as an observation.
